# Data availability

All supplied data are generated by `src/cpes_flocking/model.py` and `scripts/reproduce.py`. Canonical unit traces, summary tables, sensitivity and Monte Carlo results are directly tracked in `results/`. The model contains no measurements of real persons or real energy installations and requires no external dataset.

Source and synthetic outputs accompany version 1.0.0. The existing MIT software license is retained. The journal manuscript, editorial correspondence and publisher files are excluded from this repository package. No article DOI or archival deposit has yet been assigned in the supplied publication record.
