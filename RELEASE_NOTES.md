# Version 1.1.0 revision reproducibility source

This version accompanies the revised manuscript “A Normalized State-Space Formulation of Stress-Gated Flocking Coordination in Cyber-Physical Energy Systems” by Attila Kovari and David Raisz. The article remains under revision; no DOI is asserted.

The source corrects the target-clipping order, regenerates canonical figures and tables, and adds component ablations, one-at-a-time sensitivity sweeps and 200 initial-condition/topology trajectories. The canonical final utilization reduction under partial stress is 15.70%, with a small increase in residual stress; the result is not uniform across graph families.

All source and results are in the repository tree. No additional asset is required. Use README_RUNNING.md for installation and commands, CITATION.cff for the software citation, and manifests/SHA256SUMS.txt for integrity. The MIT license is unchanged.
